This timeline traces three strands that have developed in parallel throughout my career: professional practice, academic research, and teaching. Rather than replacing one another, these experiences have continuously informed each other — from university research and international collaborations to consultancy projects and classroom discussions. Explore the timeline to see how they intersect, overlap, and evolve over time.
