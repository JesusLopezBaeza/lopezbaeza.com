Jesús López-Baeza

Urbanist

Dr. Jesús López-Baeza's work focuses on the use of location-based data to study urban phenomena — social cohesion, livability, identity, perception, sustainability, urban growth and decline, traffic flow and pedestrian models. His contributions to new methodologies and frameworks for understanding urban dynamics have informed policy and planning decisions in cities worldwide.
