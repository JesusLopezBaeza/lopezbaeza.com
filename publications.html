Research

Publications

The publications collected here grew alongside the projects rather than independently from them. They include doctoral research at the University of Alicante, postdoctoral work at HafenCity University Hamburg, and applied research carried out with SPIN Unit. Most are co-authored with the researchers and practitioners who contributed to the underlying studies.

Across these papers runs a common ambition: translating complex urban questions into measurable evidence without losing their theoretical depth. They explore how urban form relates to social life, mobility, perception, identity, inequality and urban change by combining computational methods with empirical observation.
